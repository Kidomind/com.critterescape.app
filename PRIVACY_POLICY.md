# Privacy Policy – Critter Escape

Last updated: November 2026

Critter Escape is a puzzle game by Aksu Studio.

**Data we collect.** The game itself does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information. Game progress (such as levels and settings) is saved only on your device.

**Advertising.** If the game shows ads, they are provided by a third-party ad network (for example Google AdMob). These providers may collect device identifiers and usage data to show ads, according to their own privacy policies. Google AdMob: https://policies.google.com/privacy

**Children.** The game is not directed at children under 13.
